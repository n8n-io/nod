# Model provider logo sources

Reviewed on 2026-10-23. Provider logos are local monochrome SVG masks in `public/logos/models/`, shared by model settings and onboarding. Regional and plan variants reuse their provider’s brand. OpenCode Zen and Go retain their distinct source marks; Gemini and Vertex AI use their distinct product marks. The OpenAI-compatible server option keeps the existing generic server icon.

The 27 OpenCode assets are unmodified and covered by the accompanying `LICENSE-OPENCODE.txt` (MIT, copyright 2025 opencode). The Qwen and Ant Ling paths are unmodified from Lobe Icons; their dimensions are normalized to 24 pixels and inline layout styles removed. They are covered by `LICENSE-LOBEHUB.txt` (MIT, copyright 2023 LobeHub). These licenses cover the asset source; provider names and trademarks remain with their owners.

Radius uses the official site’s inline brand mark, with its presentation classes removed. It is attributed to Earendil Works and is not covered by either bundled MIT license. No asset loads external content, scripts or fonts.
