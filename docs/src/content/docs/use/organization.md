The **Organization** page is your chart. Otto stays one assistant. Each agent on the chart is a dossier you keep about a person. It does not speak as that person, and nothing on this page writes to a connected app.

Open it from the sidebar, or press ⌘O on a Mac and Ctrl+O elsewhere.

## What an agent is

| Kind               | What it holds                                                                                  |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Chief of staff** | Your single point of contact on the chart.                                                     |
| **Person**         | One human you manage: the notes, evidence and drafts about them.                               |
| **Upward**         | Your side of the relationship with your own manager. It prepares you. It does not assess them. |
| **Helper**         | A tool, such as a numbers pull. It stays off the chart and has no reporting line.              |

A person can report to more than one agent. The people who report to an agent are listed on that agent's dossier. A reporting line cannot loop back on itself. A helper cannot sit on a line.

## History you can see

Each dossier keeps a log:

- **Evidence**, with the link first.
- **Draft**, which stays on the dossier until you ask Otto to write it somewhere.
- **Note**, for anything else you want kept.

Otto can add to the log from chat, or from a schedule you created. Work Otto starts on its own, such as a new email, can read the dossier and cannot change it. You can add and remove entries yourself.

**Export** downloads the chart, or one dossier, as JSON. That file is the history: who reports to whom, the caps, and every entry.

## Spend caps

Each agent can have a monthly cap in US dollars. When Otto focuses that agent during a turn, the turn's model cost counts toward the cap. At the cap, Otto stops adding to that dossier and tells you. Raising the cap, or leaving it blank, lets the work continue.

This cap does not replace the limit in **Settings → Trust**. That limit still pauses Otto as a whole. See [Spending](/use/spending/).

## A feedback round

Ask in chat, or select **Ask Otto** on a dossier. Otto reads that dossier, then uses your connected apps for fresh evidence. A draft has four parts: a snapshot ranked only against the people on your chart, three sampled items, what went well with the link first, and one growth question. The draft is saved on the dossier.

Publishing it, for example to a Notion page, is a separate write. Otto asks when your words do not already cover that exact write. See [Approvals](/trust/approvals/).
