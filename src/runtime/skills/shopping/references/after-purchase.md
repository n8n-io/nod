# After a purchase

Locate the order by seller, item and purchase date. Check whether a marketplace seller, retailer or manufacturer handles the request. Read the current rules for that order and the user's country. Do not infer a legal deadline from a policy for another country.

A return, exchange, warranty repair and price adjustment have different effects. Check the refund method, return shipping cost, restocking cost and whether an exchange creates another charge. Keep the old item until the seller gives the applicable instructions.

Record the return authorization, shipping label, dispatch deadline and refund amount. A generated label does not prove that a parcel was collected. A seller's promise of a refund does not prove that the money arrived. Track those states separately.

For a dispute, preserve the order, product description, delivery record and communication. Draft a factual request with the remedy the user chose. Do not invent a defect or claim that the user returned an item.
