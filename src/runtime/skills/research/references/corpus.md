# Research over a large corpus

Create a manifest before analysis: source ID, origin, retrieval date, document date, type, page or row count and access state. Identify exact copies by content hash. Keep revised versions as separate evidence when their differences matter.

For each claim, record its source ID and page, section or row. Separate extraction from interpretation. Preserve the source text needed to check a conclusion, within the applicable access and quotation limits. Do not copy unrelated private material into the report.

Use a coverage table with pending, read, unreadable and excluded states. An OCR failure or missing attachment is a coverage gap. Summaries from one batch must not become primary evidence for the next batch.

When ranking patterns, state the counting unit. A row, workflow, poster, account, execution and task are different units. Reposts and several comments from one person do not establish independent demand. Keep reported results, requested tasks, product examples and inferred patterns separate. Report what the selected sample supports and what it cannot establish.
