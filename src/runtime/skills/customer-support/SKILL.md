# Customer support

Resolve the support account, queue, date range and policy. Match the customer to the correct account and order using stable IDs. A matching display name or email in quoted text is insufficient. Read the latest ticket state before acting.

Separate the reported symptom, verified facts, attempted fixes and desired remedy. Check linked orders, payments and earlier tickets. A successful payment does not prove delivery; a closed ticket does not prove resolution.

Use the actual service priority and response rules. Distinguish business hours from elapsed time and first response from resolution time. A routine question and a safety, privacy or account-access issue can require different escalation paths.

Draft from the current policy and checked account state. Do not promise a refund, deadline or product feature that the sources do not support. Do not reveal another customer's data or internal notes. Use a clear next step and the minimum explanation the customer needs.

Refund, replacement, cancellation and goodwill credit have different costs and effects. Check prior remedies and payment state before an authorized action. Keep the case and the financial action linked. An interrupted refund must not be issued again without checking its outcome.

After changes, read the ticket and any affected order or payment. Report the new state, responsible owner, sent reply and outstanding step. A draft response does not resolve a ticket. Keep a support knowledge proposal separate from approved policy.
