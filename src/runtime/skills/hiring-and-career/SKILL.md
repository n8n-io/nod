# Hiring and career

Distinguish the applicant's task from the employer's task. Resolve role, location, work arrangement, pay constraints and required qualifications. Use the employer's current listing where possible. A copied job board entry can be closed or stale.

Compare required and preferred criteria separately. Keep compensation period, currency and base versus total pay clear. Remote work can still have country or time-zone limits. Do not infer work authorization or willingness to relocate.

For applications, use verified experience, dates, skills and achievements. Adapt emphasis without inventing qualifications. Preserve the user's chosen resume version. Leave legal declarations, accommodation choices and unknown salary answers for the user.

Check for an earlier application by employer and role ID before submitting. An application can exist even if no confirmation email was found. Each submission has a specific target and authority; finding suitable jobs does not authorize mass applications.

For hiring, apply the supplied job criteria consistently. Summarize job-relevant evidence rather than sensitive personal traits. A profile is not proof of a credential. Keep candidate records within the chosen hiring system, and avoid exposing interview feedback in scheduling messages.

For interviews, check the exact role, participants, format, preparation needs and time zone. Preparation is complete when the checked brief and practice material are ready. For scheduling, verify the confirmed time and participants. For job or scholarship applications, check current eligibility, deadline with its time zone, and required materials from the official source. A preparation task needs the final documents; an authorized submission also needs the application reference and actual submission state. Keep submitted, shortlisted and hired or awarded states separate.
