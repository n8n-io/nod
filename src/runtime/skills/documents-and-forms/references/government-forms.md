# Government applications

Find the form through the responsible authority's website. Check the form edition, country, office, applicant type and applicable submission route. A commercial preparation service can look like an official portal and charge a separate fee.

Map facts to the exact field meanings. Country of citizenship, country of residence and place of birth are different facts. Preserve the official transliteration from the user's documents. Use the form's required date format while retaining the verified date. Ask the user to resolve an ambiguous source date before filling the field. Do not guess a previous address, immigration status, offence history or eligibility declaration.

List missing evidence with the field or requirement it supports. Check current translation, certification, photo, file-size and document-validity requirements from the authority. Do not assume that rules for another application type apply.

Keep drafting separate from signing, paying and submitting. Show the user the final declarations and unresolved answers that require their knowledge. After an authorized submission, preserve the application reference, submitted copy and next official step. A portal receipt is not an approval, and an appointment request is not an appointment.
