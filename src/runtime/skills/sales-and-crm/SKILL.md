# Sales and CRM

Resolve the CRM, account owner, pipeline and target segment. Use the business's stage definitions and qualification rules. A lead is not an opportunity, and a contact can belong to more than one account.

Read the current record before proposing changes. Match records by stable ID, verified domain and contact details. Do not merge people because their names match. Preserve source and confidence for enrichment. A public email address does not prove that it belongs to the intended person.

For lead capture, separate an inquiry, a qualified lead and consent to receive marketing. Before sending marketing messages, check the applicable consent for the selected channel and the current opt-out or suppression state. Do not send when consent is required but missing, or when the recipient has opted out. Keep the original source and relevant message. A form submission can be spam or an existing customer request. Do not turn a general research task into bulk outreach.

For meeting preparation, connect the calendar attendee to the correct account. Use the latest CRM notes, open issues and actual communication. Separate a buying signal from an inference. Do not invent a budget, intent or decision-maker role.

For pipeline analysis, use a clear snapshot date, currency and stage basis. Separate booked revenue from weighted forecasts. Flag stale close dates, missing next steps and duplicate opportunities. Compare changes by record ID rather than by totals alone.

For authorized updates or outreach, check the final record or sent message. Keep recipients, owner and stage correct. A renewal draft is not an agreed renewal. Report records changed, unresolved matches and evidence for each consequential change. Use a limited sample before a large update and stop on a mapping error.
